## 3. Required information for each image

For every image, record as much of the following information as possible:

- **Image ID and path:** a stable identifier and the path within the repository.
- **Original source:** the original file page or source URL, not only a search result.
- **Creator and rights holder:** names as supplied by the source, where available.
- **Licence:** exact name, version and URL, or a description of the applicable public-domain status or written permission.
- **Evidence:** a link to the licence statement, archived page or permission record.
- **Verification date:** the date on which the rights were reviewed.
- **Modifications:** cropping, resizing, colour changes or other edits.
- **Restrictions and notes:** any additional conditions relevant to redistribution.

Do not publish private correspondence or personal contact details without permission. Permission records may be retained privately where necessary, with a public summary of the scope of the permission.

## 4. Attribution format

Where attribution is required, use the following format when the information is available:

> Title or image ID — Creator, source URL, licence name and version, licence URL. Changes made: [description, or "none"].

For images with ShareAlike, NonCommercial or other conditions, attribution alone is not sufficient. All applicable licence terms must be respected.

## 5. Unclear or disputed rights

Images with missing, contradictory or unclear licensing information must not be treated as unrestricted.

If a rights issue is reported, the image may be temporarily removed from public distribution while its status is reviewed.

## 6. Dataset and model use

Permission to redistribute an image does not necessarily resolve every legal question concerning machine-learning training, derived datasets, privacy, third-party rights or commercial use.

Users are responsible for checking the rights and conditions applicable to their intended use.

## 7. Contact and corrections

If you are a rights holder, or have evidence that an image's licence or attribution is incorrect, please open an issue in this repository or contact the project maintainer.

Please include the relevant image ID and supporting information.
